1. Independent Sales Partner (Cybersecurity)

The Deal: 20% Recurring Revenue Share on Every Client You Bring & Service

We are looking for independent sales partners in cybersecurity to bring in and commercially manage clients for Sir Revizor Continuous Code Security Monitoring (independent daily code audits and continuous monitoring across the client's entire language stack).

  • 20% Recurring Commission Under a Written Agreement: You earn 20% of revenue from every payment actually received from every client you bring in, for as long as you handle commercial account management for that client.
  • Pre-Qualified Public Open-Source Repositories: No blind cold-pitching of vaporware. Every prospect in your brief is an active company whose public open-source GitHub repository we analyzed locally on an isolated fork under coordinated disclosure rules (never touching live production systems or private code without authorization).
  • Free Technical Demo on Live Code: To close deals cleanly, every qualified prospect can receive a free one-time technical breakdown on their public code — sent directly by the technical founder to the client's designated official security contact (sensitive bug details are never handed to sales reps).
  • Ready-to-Use Sales Playbook: You receive a complete outreach playbook, sanitized sample reports (Solidity & Rust/WASM), pricing terms, and full technical backing to move conversations from first touch to subscription agreement.

Transparent Written Partner Terms & Account Servicing Definition

Before starting on your first batch of 10–15 pre-qualified leads, we sign a clear private Independent Sales Agent Agreement specifying:

  • What "Servicing the Account" Means (4 Concrete Duties): Because the technical founder runs the direct engineering channel with the client's developers, your ongoing 20% commission is tied to four commercial duties: (1) coordinating timely monthly subscription renewals and crypto payment collection, (2) handling commercial upsells when the client adds repositories or chains, (3) gathering client feedback and permission for case studies/references, and (4) handling live voice/commercial calls in English when needed.
  • Clear Handover & Termination Rule: Your 20% recurring share continues for as long as the client pays and you perform the commercial servicing above (logged in the leads register). It stops only if you abandon the account (60 days of silence without valid reason, a client request for a new account manager, or voluntary exit with a 14-day handover notice).
  • Payouts & Lead Protection: Calculated on net collected revenue; paid in crypto with written transaction confirmations (txid). Each registered lead has one exclusive owner — no internal poaching and a strict "one refusal = stop forever" anti-spam rule.

What We Expect From You

  • Proven B2B sales experience in software, infrastructure, or cybersecurity.
  • Fluent spoken and written English with solid command of technical terminology.
  • Ability to reach and hold credible conversations with CTOs, heads of security, and technical founders.

Zero Bureaucracy to Start

  • No interviews.
  • No entrance tests.
  • No rigid employment commitments.
  • Send one concise line about your background via email or Telegram DM, pick up the playbook and initial prospect brief, and start on real accounts.

2. Business Co-Founder (Open via Sales Partner Track)

Co-Foundership Proven by Real Client Results

Beyond the 20% recurring commission track, the position of Business Co-Founder — taking ownership of the commercial side, strategic partnerships, fundraising, and the future company structure alongside the technical founder — is open to candidates who demonstrate their capability in action as sales partners.

We do not hire co-founders through pitch decks or multi-stage interview panels. Co-foundership is proven by tangible results in real work with clients, and the strongest performing partners on the sales track are considered directly for the Business Co-Founder role.
